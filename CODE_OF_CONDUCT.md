# Code of Conduct

PhishHawk follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, be respectful, assume good faith, and focus on what is
best for the people who use the tool. Harassment, personal attacks and
publishing other people's private information are not acceptable. That includes
posting real emails that contain someone else's address or data.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour in this project's issues, pull requests or discussions
worries you, report it privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/vinitrami-Soc/phishhawk/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting [@vinitrami-Soc](https://github.com/vinitrami-Soc). Reports are
handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove content, or block people who
break these rules, as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
